From hectares of land in Enugu State, Red Based Agric Farm has grown into one of Nigerian most trusted farm-direct brands — built on soil, sweat, and an uncompromising commitment to quality

WHY WE EXIST

Farm-direct quality has always been possible

Nigerian households deserve to know where their food comes from. For decades, palm oil passed through so many hands between farm and kitchen that quality, freshness, and transparency were lost along the way.

We started Red Based Agric to change that. By selling directly to consumers, no agent, no warehousing delays, we guarantee freshness, traceability, and fair pricing for everyone involved

Red Based Agric Farm Limited was incorporated in Nigeria to carry out the business of organic farming, agricultural produce, and the processing and packaging of red palm oil for export markets. We don't just grow crops — we grow businesses, economies, and futures for sustainability.
