From a single plot of land in Benue State, RedBased Agric Farm has grown into one of Nigeria's most trusted farm-direct brands — built on soil, sweat, and an uncompromising commitment to quality

WHY WE EXIST

Farm-direct quality has always been possible

Nigerian households deserve to know where their food comes from. For decades, palm oil passed through so many hands between farm and kitchen that quality, freshness, and transparency were lost along the way.

We started RedBased Agric to change that. By selling directly to consumers, no middlemen, no warehousing delays, we guarantee freshness, traceability, and fair pricing for everyone involved

OUR JOURNEY

Milestones

2019
Founded in Benue State

Jane Lewis starts RedBased Agric Farm with 8 hectares and a mission: better palm oil for Nigerian kitchens

2020
NAFDAC Certification

After 14 months of rigorous inspections and lab testing, we earn our NAFDAC food production licence

2021
Online Store Launch

We launch our e-commerce platform and complete our first long-distance delivery to Lagos

2022
Cold-Press Technology

We invest in hydraulic cold-press extraction equipment, preserving β-carotene and Vitamin E in every batch

2023
Nationwide Delivery

Partnership with courier networks enables delivery to all 36 states and the FCT

2024
Farm Subscription Launch

Monthly farm boxes launch to serve families who want consistent supply without repeat ordering

2025
Farmer Training Programme

We launch a quarterly training programme for 120+ smallholder farmers in our district

2026
5,000+ Happy Customers

Our community of loyal customers grows — and we're just getting started
